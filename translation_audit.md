# 38 篇缓存翻译逐篇试译与覆盖审计

范围：`parsed_papers/*.json` 中的 38 个 `translation` 字段，按文件名排序。英文为依据缓存中文的**试译**，不是官方参考答案；方法标签见 [`translation_methods.md`](translation_methods.md)。本轮不修改题库原文。`待核` 指缓存文字或事实存在疑点，不以猜测代替原卷核对。

## 2020 年：6 篇

### 2020_12_1｜北京大兴国际机场

Beijing Daxing International Airport, located 46 kilometers south of Tiananmen Square, opened on September 30, 2019. Construction of this massive project began in 2014, with more than 40,000 workers on site at its peak. The terminal's compact design allows as many aircraft as possible to park near its center, making travel much more convenient for passengers. Although the terminal has 82 boarding gates, passengers can reach any of them in less than eight minutes after passing through security. The airport is designed to handle 300 aircraft takeoffs and landings per hour. Its annual passenger traffic is expected to reach 100 million by 2040, making it a potential contender for the world's busiest airport.

方法：地点后置、过去时与未来预期、分句、结果关系。新增提醒：`300架次起降` 可明确为 aircraft movements；“有望”不能译成已成事实。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“位于天安门广场以南46公里处，于2019年9月30日投入使用。”** → `Beijing Daxing International Airport, located 46 kilometers south of Tiananmen Square, opened on September 30, 2019.` 主干先定为“机场投入使用”，用 `opened` 表达启用；`located...` 补地理位置，不另造一个“机场位于”的重复主句。`south of` 给出相对方位，`46 kilometers` 和日期必须原样核对。
2. **“2014年开工，高峰时有4万多工人。”** → `Construction of this massive project began in 2014, with more than 40,000 workers on site at its peak.` 英文让“工程建设”作主语，`began` 对应明确过去时间；后半句是同一工程的施工规模，用 `with...` 补充。`more than` 保留“多”，`at its peak` 限定人数只在高峰时期。
3. **“航站楼设计紧凑……给乘客极大方便。”** → `The terminal's compact design allows as many aircraft as possible to park near its center, making travel much more convenient for passengers.` 主干是“紧凑设计允许飞机停靠”，`as many...as possible` 处理“最大数量”；结尾的 `making...` 表示这种设计带来的便利，逻辑主语是前面整个设计效果。原文还有“直接停靠”，试译主要保留了靠近中心这一关键位置。
4. **“共有82个登机口，但安检后不到8分钟可到任何一个。”** → `Although the terminal has 82 boarding gates, passengers can reach any of them in less than eight minutes after passing through security.` “但”表让步，用 `Although...` 先交代门多，再让“乘客能到达”成为主句；`after passing through security` 限定计时起点。`any of them` 指82个登机口，`less than` 不能写成“约8分钟”。
5. **“设计可确保每小时300架次起降。”** → `The airport is designed to handle 300 aircraft takeoffs and landings per hour.` 先写设计能力，不把“可确保”误译为每小时实际都发生300次；`per hour` 保留速率。`架次起降` 计的是起飞或降落的航班动作，若需更严谨可用 `300 aircraft movements per hour`，避免理解成300架飞机各起飞又降落一次。
6. **“2040年年客运量将达1亿，有望成为最繁忙机场。”** → `Its annual passenger traffic is expected to reach 100 million by 2040, making it a potential contender for the world's busiest airport.` `is expected to reach` 表预测而非既成事实；`annual` 对应“年”，`by 2040` 对应截止时间。后半句仍是可能性，用 `potential contender` 保留“有望”，不能断言现在就是世界最繁忙机场。

### 2020_12_2｜港珠澳大桥

The 55-kilometer Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge is a remarkable engineering achievement in China. Connecting three cities, it forms the world's longest sea-crossing bridge-and-tunnel system. It has cut travel time between the cities from three hours to 30 minutes. This enormous reinforced-concrete bridge demonstrates China's ability to build record-breaking megastructures. It will promote regional integration and economic growth. The bridge is central to China's overall plan for developing its own Greater Bay Area. China hopes to turn the area into a region that can rival the bay areas of San Francisco, New York and Tokyo in technological innovation and economic prosperity.

方法：数字作定语、主动与分词、`from...to...`、目的／愿景。新增提醒：`连接三城` 是功能，`缩短时间` 是结果；勿把所有将来动作写成既成事实。

#### 逐句解析（样例）

1. **“全长55公里，是……工程壮举。”** → `The 55-kilometer Hong Kong–Zhuhai–Macao Bridge is a remarkable engineering achievement in China.` 先找主干“大桥是壮举”，桥名作主语，`is` 连表语；“全长55公里”改为桥名前的复合形容词 `55-kilometer`，数字后的单位用单数。题干给出的英文名写 `Macau`，本试译写 `Macao`；考场应优先照用题干给词，不要自行改拼写。
2. **“连接三个城市，是世界上最长的……系统。”** → `Connecting three cities, it forms the world's longest sea-crossing bridge-and-tunnel system.` 两条信息都围绕“大桥”，先用 `it forms...` 立主干，再把“连接三城”压成句首的 `Connecting...`；其逻辑主语仍是桥。`bridge-and-tunnel system` 将“桥梁和隧道”作为一个系统，而不是误写成两项分别都最长。
3. **“旅行时间从3小时缩短到30分钟。”** → `It has cut travel time between the cities from three hours to 30 minutes.` 先写“桥缩短了旅行时间”，再用 `from...to...` 准确保留前后两个数值。`has cut` 着重已实现的结果对现在仍有影响；不能把“3小时”或“30分钟”漏掉。
4. **“跨度巨大的钢筋混凝土大桥证明中国有能力……”** → `This enormous reinforced-concrete bridge demonstrates China's ability to build record-breaking megastructures.` 主干是“桥证明能力”，`demonstrates China's ability to...` 比逐字写“充分证明中国是有能力的”紧凑。现译的 `enormous` 只概括“大”，没有明确表达“跨度”；若要求信息更全，可改为 `This long-span reinforced-concrete bridge...`。
5. **“它将助推区域一体化，促进经济增长。”** → `It will promote regional integration and economic growth.` “将”指预期作用，所以用 `will`；两个宾语共享 `promote`，不必为了对应两个中文动词而重复写两个英文谓语。
6. **“大桥是……总体规划的关键。”** → `The bridge is central to China's overall plan for developing its own Greater Bay Area.` 先翻判断主干“大桥是关键”，`is central to` 自然表达“对……至关重要”；`for developing...` 再说明这是什么规划。不要把“规划”误当成已经建成的结果。
7. **“中国希望将大湾区建成……相媲美的地区。”** → `China hopes to turn the area into a region that can rival the bay areas of San Francisco, New York and Tokyo in technological innovation and economic prosperity.` 主干先写“中国希望把地区变成……”：`China hopes to turn the area into...`；“能与……相媲美”修饰目标地区，用 `that can rival...`；`in...` 限定比较的是技术创新和经济繁荣，而不是地理面积。“希望”必须保留，不能写成大湾区现在已经达到这一水平。

### 2020_12_3｜青藏铁路

The Qinghai–Tibet Railway is the world's highest and longest railway built on a plateau. It stretches 1,956 kilometers, 960 of which run at an altitude of over 4,000 meters, and is the first railway linking Tibet with the rest of China. Because the line passes through one of the world's most fragile ecosystems, measures were taken both during and after construction to protect the environment and make it a “green railway.” The railway has greatly reduced travel time to Tibet. More importantly, it has boosted Tibet's economy and improved local people's lives. Since the line opened, more and more people have chosen to travel to Tibet by train, giving themselves a chance to enjoy the scenery along the way.

方法：数字和单位、原因与目的、被动、`since` 后的时态。新增提醒：`960公里` 的指代应清楚，不要误写成整条铁路都在 4,000 米以上。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“青藏铁路是世界上最高最长的高原铁路。”** → `The Qinghai–Tibet Railway is the world's highest and longest railway built on a plateau.` 先立判断主干“铁路是……铁路”，把两个最高级并列；`built on a plateau` 限定比较对象为高原铁路。不要把“最高”和“最长”合成一个含义不明的形容词。
2. **“全长1956公里，其中960公里海拔4000米以上，是连接西藏与其他地区的第一条铁路。”** → `It stretches 1,956 kilometers, 960 of which run at an altitude of over 4,000 meters, and is the first railway linking Tibet with the rest of China.` 先给总长，再用 `960 of which` 只说明其中一段；`over 4,000 meters` 不可误套到全线。最后的 `the first railway linking...` 补铁路功能与“第一条”，数字、范围、序数都须保留。
3. **“由于穿越脆弱生态系统……采取保护措施，以确保成为绿色铁路。”** → `Because the line passes through one of the world's most fragile ecosystems, measures were taken both during and after construction to protect the environment and make it a “green railway.”` “由于”先标原因，主干“采取了措施”没有施事者，转成 `measures were taken`；`both during and after` 保留建设期间与建成后两个时段；两个 `to` 目标分别是保护环境与建设绿色铁路。
4. **“大大缩短了去往西藏的旅行时间。”** → `The railway has greatly reduced travel time to Tibet.` 让铁路作自然主语，`greatly` 保留“大大”；现在完成时突出开通后的持续效果。这里的对象是“去往西藏的旅行时间”，不是西藏内部所有行程。
5. **“更重要的是，促进经济发展，改善居民生活。”** → `More importantly, it has boosted Tibet's economy and improved local people's lives.` `More importantly` 保留原文的强调层级；铁路这一共同主语后，两个并列动词分别对应经济与民生，不必机械改成非谓语。
6. **“开通后，愈来愈多人乘火车前往西藏，还能欣赏沿线美景。”** → `Since the line opened, more and more people have chosen to travel to Tibet by train, giving themselves a chance to enjoy the scenery along the way.` `Since` 指从开通延续至今，主句用现在完成时；`more and more` 保留人数增长。`giving...` 是乘车旅行带来的附加机会，而不是说所有乘客必定都在赏景。

### 2020_7_1｜《三国演义》

*The Romance of the Three Kingdoms* is a famous Chinese historical novel written in the fourteenth century. Set in the Three Kingdoms period, it depicts the wars among the states of Wei, Shu and Wu from the latter half of the second century to the latter half of the third century. The novel portrays nearly a thousand characters and countless historical events. Although most are based on real history, they have been romanticized and dramatized to varying degrees. Widely recognized as a literary masterpiece, the novel has attracted generation after generation of readers since its publication and has had a broad, lasting influence on Chinese culture.

方法：作品名、过去分词作定语、让步、现在完成时。新增提醒：文学作品的 `set in`、`depicts`、`portrays` 和 `romanticized` 属于课堂例题之外的题材词汇。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“《三国演义》是历史小说，写于十四世纪。”** → `The Romance of the Three Kingdoms is a famous Chinese historical novel written in the fourteenth century.` 书名作主语，`is a...novel` 先完成判断；“写于十四世纪”用 `written...` 作后置定语，修饰小说，不必再写一个重复的被动句。题干已给英文书名，应优先核对并沿用。
2. **“以三国历史为背景，描写二世纪下半叶至三世纪下半叶的战争。”** → `Set in the Three Kingdoms period, it depicts the wars among the states of Wei, Shu and Wu from the latter half of the second century to the latter half of the third century.` `Set in...` 交代故事背景，后面的 `it` 指小说，避免悬垂；主干是“小说描写战争”。`from...to...` 精确连接起止时间，“魏蜀吴”三方和两个“下半叶”都要保留。
3. **“刻画了近千个人物和无数历史事件。”** → `The novel portrays nearly a thousand characters and countless historical events.` 主语延续“小说”，`portrays` 适合描述文学作品塑造人物；`nearly` 对应“近”，不能写成恰好一千。人物和事件两类宾语并列，不能只译故事情节。
4. **“这些人物和事件虽然基于真实历史，但被不同程度地浪漫化、戏剧化。”** → `Although most are based on real history, they have been romanticized and dramatized to varying degrees.` 先识别“虽然……但……”的让步关系，英文有 `Although` 就不再另加 `but`；`most` 回指前句人物和事件。后半句用被动，因为人物与事件是被艺术加工的对象；`to varying degrees` 保留“不同程度”。
5. **“公认的杰作；面世以来吸引读者并影响文化。”** → `Widely recognized as a literary masterpiece, the novel has attracted generation after generation of readers since its publication and has had a broad, lasting influence on Chinese culture.` 把“公认杰作”压成句首修饰，主语紧跟 `the novel`；`since its publication` 把时间延续到现在，所以两个主要动作都用现在完成时。`generation after generation` 对应“一代又一代”，`broad, lasting` 分别保留影响的范围与持续性。

### 2020_9_1｜《西游记》【待核原文】

*Journey to the West* is perhaps the most influential of China's Four Great Classical Novels and certainly one of the best known abroad. It recounts the arduous journey of the monk Xuanzang, accompanied by three disciples, through western China to India in search of Buddhist scriptures. Although the story has a Buddhist theme, the novel draws heavily on Chinese folktales and mythology to create a host of vivid human and animal characters. The best known is Sun Wukong, whose battles with all manner of demons are familiar to almost every Chinese child.

方法：最高级、让步、人物与作品介绍。新增提醒：缓存为“玄奖”“取竺”，这里暂按语境译作“玄奘”“取经”；应核对原卷后才可把修正写入题库。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“也许是四大经典小说中最具影响力的一部，也是国外最广为人知的一部。”** → `Journey to the West is perhaps the most influential of China's Four Great Classical Novels and certainly one of the best known abroad.` 书名作主语，两个评价并列；`perhaps` 保留“也许”，不把推测写成定论。`the most influential of...` 和 `one of the best known...` 分别处理两个最高级范围，不要漏掉“在国外”。
2. **“僧侣……由三个随从陪同，穿越中国西部前往印度取……”** → `It recounts the arduous journey of the monk Xuanzang, accompanied by three disciples, through western China to India in search of Buddhist scriptures.` 主干是“小说讲述艰难旅程”，后面按人物、陪同者、路线、目的逐层补充；`in search of...` 表去印度的目的。缓存疑似 OCR 把“玄奘／取经”写成“玄奖／取竺”，试译按语境暂作此理解，仍须核对原卷；`three disciples` 比原文“随从”更具体，保守译法可用 `three companions`。
3. **“虽然主题基于佛教，但大量采用民间故事和神话素材，创造形象。”** → `Although the story has a Buddhist theme, the novel draws heavily on Chinese folktales and mythology to create a host of vivid human and animal characters.` `Although` 标出让步，主句说作品实际取材；`to create...` 说明这些素材被用来做什么。`human and animal characters` 分别保留人物与动物形象，`vivid` 对应“栩栩如生”。
4. **“最著名的是孙悟空，其斗妖故事几乎人人熟知。”** → `The best known is Sun Wukong, whose battles with all manner of demons are familiar to almost every Chinese child.` 主干先点出人物，`whose...` 补充孙悟空的故事；`almost` 对应“几乎”，`every Chinese child` 限定熟悉者，不能夸成全世界所有人。

### 2020_9_2｜《水浒传》

*Water Margin* is one of China's Four Great Classical Novels. Based on the story of the historical figure Song Jiang and his companions rebelling against a feudal ruler, it has been loved by Chinese readers for centuries. It is no exaggeration to say that almost everyone in China knows some of its main characters. Its memorable stories have been retold in teahouses, on theater stages, on radio and television, on cinema screens and in countless homes. In fact, the novel's influence has extended far beyond China's borders. A growing number of readers overseas also find its stories vivid, moving and entertaining.

方法：分词、持续至今的时态、并列信息。新增提醒：中文媒介列举较长，可压成并列短语，但不能漏掉影响走向海外这一信息。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“《水浒传》是四大经典小说之一。”** → `Water Margin is one of China's Four Great Classical Novels.` 作品名作主语，`one of...` 后接复数 `Novels`；“之一”必须保留，不能写成它就是唯一或最重要的一部。
2. **“基于宋江及伙伴反抗封建帝王的故事，数百年来受喜爱。”** → `Based on the story of the historical figure Song Jiang and his companions rebelling against a feudal ruler, it has been loved by Chinese readers for centuries.` `Based on...` 先交代题材来源，后面的 `it` 指小说；主干“它一直受读者喜爱”用现在完成时被动，`for centuries` 表持续时间。现译 `ruler` 比原文“帝王”稍宽，若需紧贴原字可考虑 `feudal emperor`。
3. **“毫不夸张地说，几乎每个中国人都熟悉主要人物。”** → `It is no exaggeration to say that almost everyone in China knows some of its main characters.` `It is no exaggeration to say that...` 整体对应开头语气；`almost` 保留“几乎”，`some of` 保留“其中一些”，不夸大为每个人认识全部角色。
4. **“精彩故事在茶馆、舞台、广播电视、电影和家庭反复讲述。”** → `Its memorable stories have been retold in teahouses, on theater stages, on radio and television, on cinema screens and in countless homes.` 故事是被讲述的对象，故用被动；现在完成时表示延续至今的传播。长媒介列举可以压成并列短语，但需逐项对应；地点不同，介词分别用 `in`、`on`，不必强求一致。
5. **“事实上，影响已远超国界。”** → `In fact, the novel's influence has extended far beyond China's borders.` `In fact` 承接并加强判断；让“影响”作主语，`has extended` 写影响扩散到现在。`far beyond` 保留“远远超出”，不要只译成“在国外有影响”。
6. **“越来越多的外国读者觉得故事生动感人、趣味盎然。”** → `A growing number of readers overseas also find its stories vivid, moving and entertaining.` `A growing number of...` 表人数增长；`find + 宾语 + 形容词` 把评价直接接在故事后面。三个形容词分别保留“生动、感人、有趣”，`overseas` 与上一句“超出国界”相呼应。

### 2021_12_1｜中共一大会址

The site of the First National Congress of the Communist Party of China, at 76 Xingye Road in Shanghai, is a typical Shanghai-style residence built in the autumn of 1920. The Congress convened there on July 23, 1921. It adopted the Party's first program and resolution, elected its central leadership and marked the birth of the Communist Party of China. The site was restored in September 1952, when a memorial museum was established and opened to the public. Besides introducing the Congress's delegates, the museum traces the Party's historical development. Today it serves as a center for patriotic education, where visitors can learn about Party history and remember revolutionary martyrs.

方法：地点、按时间推进、并列动词、被动。新增提醒：`site`、`Congress`、`program`、`resolution` 要分清，不能都译为泛泛的 `meeting` 或 `document`。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“中共一大会址位于……建于1920年秋。”** → `The site of the First National Congress... is a typical Shanghai-style residence built in the autumn of 1920.` 先用 `The site ... is a residence` 搭起主系表；地址用 `at 76 Xingye Road in Shanghai` 插入，建造时间用过去分词 `built` 后置。`site` 指会址，不能与大会本身混同。
2. **“1921年7月23日……在此召开。”** → `The Congress convened there on July 23, 1921.` 日期放句尾；历史事件用一般过去时。这里 `Congress` 回指上句已写全称的大会，避免重复冗长名称。
3. **“大会通过……选举……宣告……”** → `It adopted..., elected... and marked...` 同一个主语连用三个过去式动词，按原文事件顺序推进。`program` 是纲领，`resolution` 是决议；`marked the birth` 表达“标志着诞生”，不必机械地给“宣告”另造主语。
4. **“1952年9月……修复，建立纪念馆并对外开放。”** → `The site was restored..., when a memorial museum was established and opened...` 会址与纪念馆分别作被动句主语，明确谁被修复、谁被建立开放；`when` 把同一时段的动作接起来。
5. **“纪念馆除了介绍……还介绍……”** → `Besides introducing..., the museum traces...` `Besides + -ing` 处理“除了……还”；主句保留 `the museum traces` 这一明确主谓，不把“介绍”重复两遍。
6. **“现已成为……基地。”** → `Today it serves as a center for patriotic education, where...` `serves as` 说当前功能，`where` 再解释游客在这里了解党史、缅怀先烈；比把所有信息塞进一个长名词更清楚。

3. **“西高东低，河流众多，形成……峡谷。”** → `Higher in the west than in the east, the plateau has many rivers that have carved out...` 比较短语前置，主句保留 `the plateau has`；`that... carved out` 明确峡谷由河流形成。
4. **“峡谷中……土壤肥沃……有利于农作物生长。”** → `The fertile soil... is well suited to growing...` 主语选肥沃土壤；`be suited to + -ing` 中的 `to` 是介词，不接动词原形。
5. **“独特自然环境造就……多样性。”** → `The plateau's distinctive natural environment has fostered...` 因在前、果在后；`biological and cultural diversity` 分别对应生物和文化。
6. **“森林和矿产资源……古人类起源……”** → `It is rich in... and is also an important area in the study of...` 两个 `is` 连接资源与研究意义。试译把“古人类起源的重要地区”谨慎表述为“研究古人类起源的重要地区”，这是解释性处理，原意需对照原卷。
7. **“少数民族数量最多……各民族保留传统。”** → `More ethnic minority groups live there than in any other region of China, and each has preserved...` 用比较结构明确“最多”的全国范围；`each` 指每个民族，谓语用单数 `has`。

### 2022_6_1｜卢沟桥

The Lugou Bridge, 15 kilometers southwest of Tiananmen Square, spans the Yongding River and is Beijing's oldest surviving multi-arch stone bridge. First completed in 1192 and rebuilt in 1698, it is supported by 281 pillars, each topped with a stone lion. More lions are carved into the heads, backs, bellies and paws of these statues. Lifelike and varied in form, the lions are masterpieces of the bridge's stone-carving art. There are so many that a local saying claims no one can count the stone lions on the Lugou Bridge. The bridge is known not only for its beauty but also as a milestone in the history of stone-bridge construction.

方法：位置、先后时间、被动、`not only...but also...`。新增提醒：桥名可用 `Lugou Bridge`，也常见 `Marco Polo Bridge`；本题保留中文拼音称呼即可。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“位于……横跨……是……石桥。”** → `The Lugou Bridge, 15 kilometers southwest of..., spans... and is...` 方位以插入语紧跟桥名；主干两个并列谓语 `spans` 和 `is`，同时说功能与身份。
2. **“1192年建成，1698年重建……281根柱子……每根有石狮。”** → `First completed... and rebuilt..., it is supported..., each topped...` 建造年代用过去分词前置，支撑结构用被动，`each topped with` 补充每根柱子的装饰。要使 `each` 明确回指 `pillars`。
3. **“石狮的头、背、腹或爪子上还藏着更多狮子。”** → `More lions are carved into...` 用被动让被雕刻的小狮子作主语；四个身体部位并列，不把“藏着”直译成物理躲藏。
4. **“生动逼真、千姿百态，是……精品。”** → `Lifelike and varied in form, the lions are masterpieces...` 形容词短语前置，主干 `the lions are masterpieces`；“精品”可译 `masterpieces`，不必另写泛泛的 `very beautiful`。
5. **“不计其数，因而流传……说法。”** → `There are so many that a local saying claims...` `so...that` 呈现数量与俗语的因果；俗语按意思转述，未伪装成固定英文原话。
6. **“不仅以美学特征闻名……还是建筑史丰碑。”** → `The bridge is known not only for... but also as...` `for` 接闻名原因，`as` 接被视作的身份；两半并列结构的介词不同，但逻辑对应。

### 2022_6_2｜南京长江大桥

The Nanjing Yangtze River Bridge was the first combined road-and-rail bridge over the Yangtze River to be designed by China and built with domestically produced materials. Its upper, four-lane road deck is 4,589 meters long, while its lower, double-track rail deck is 6,772 meters long. The rail bridge connects the former Tianjin–Pukou and Shanghai–Nanjing railway lines, cutting the time needed for trains to cross the river from an hour and a half to two minutes. The bridge is an important transport hub between northern and southern China and one of Nanjing's best-known attractions. Its completion marked a leap forward in Chinese bridge construction. It greatly facilitated the movement of goods and people across the river, promoting economic development and improving people's lives.

方法：上下层信息拆分、数字、结果。新增提醒：`road-and-rail bridge`、`four-lane`、`double-track` 是复合修饰语；两个长度不可颠倒。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“首座……铁路、公路两用桥。”** → `The Nanjing Yangtze River Bridge was the first combined road-and-rail bridge...` 主干先写身份；`to be designed... and built...` 后置说明“中国设计、国产材料建造”。`first` 限定的是题干这组完整条件，不宜扩大成“世界第一”。
2. **“上层四车道……下层双轨道……各长……”** → `Its upper... is 4,589 meters long, while its lower... is 6,772...` 用 `while` 对照上下层；`four-lane`、`double-track` 放名词前，两个数字分别对应公路桥与铁路桥。
3. **“连接两条铁路……过江时间从……缩短到……”** → `The rail bridge connects..., cutting the time... from... to...` 主句说连接，分词说结果；`from...to...` 保持一个半小时到两分钟的方向。
4. **“是南北交通枢纽……著名景点。”** → `The bridge is an important transport hub... and one of...` 两个名词性表语并列；`one of` 后的景点用复数 `attractions`。
5. **“建成标志着桥梁建设飞跃。”** → `Its completion marked a leap forward...` 把“建成”名词化为主语 `completion`，动词 `marked` 正好承担“标志”。
6. **“方便物资、人员往来……促进经济……改善生活。”** → `It greatly facilitated..., promoting... and improving...` 主句写直接作用，两个分词写进一步影响；`goods and people` 对应物资与人员，不把两者混成单一交通量。

### 2022_6_3｜赵州桥

The Zhaozhou Bridge was built during the Sui dynasty, around AD 605. It is 50.82 meters long, 9.6 meters wide and has a span of 37.37 meters. The ingenious architect Li Chun designed the bridge and supervised its construction. Novel in structure and graceful in appearance, it has a large main arch and smaller arches at either end. These help release floodwater, reduce the bridge's weight and save stone. Since its completion, the bridge has survived many floods and earthquakes, yet its main structure remains intact and it is still in use. It was a pioneering achievement in the history of bridge building and an outstanding accomplishment of ancient Chinese civilization. Bridges of similar design did not appear in Europe until the fourteenth century, more than 700 years later.

方法：时间、尺寸、功能并列、让步与持续状态。新增提醒：不要把 `span` 误作整桥长度；“直到……才”用 `not...until`。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“建于隋朝，公元605年左右。”** → `The Zhaozhou Bridge was built during the Sui dynasty, around AD 605.` 桥作被建造对象，所以用被动；朝代与约略年份互相补充，`around` 保留“左右”。
2. **“长……宽……跨度……”** → `It is... long,... wide and has a span of...` 长宽用 `be + 数字 + 形容词`，跨度用 `have a span of`；`span` 不是全桥长度。
3. **“李春设计并监督建设。”** → `Li Chun designed... and supervised...` 人物作主语，两个一般过去时谓语并列；`construction` 名词化“建设”，避免重复 `bridge`。
4. **“结构新颖……有一大拱和两端小拱。”** → `Novel in structure and graceful in appearance, it has...` 描述性短语前置，主句仍要有 `it has`；`at either end` 对应大拱两端。
5. **“小拱帮助排洪、减重、节省石材。”** → `These help release..., reduce... and save...` `These` 回指小拱，三个动词原形跟在 `help` 后并列，动作目的清楚。
6. **“建成以来……多次洪水地震，但仍完好、使用。”** → `Since its completion, the bridge has survived..., yet... remains...` `since` 配现在完成时总结经历，`yet` 引出到现在仍保持的状态；`is still in use` 是当前持续使用。
7. **“是……创举……杰出成就。”** → `It was a pioneering achievement... and an outstanding accomplishment...` 两个评价并列，明确一个是世界桥梁史，一个是中国古代文明史。
8. **“直到14世纪欧洲才出现……晚700多年。”** → `Bridges of similar design did not appear... until..., more than 700 years later.` `not...until` 处理“直到……才”，末尾时间差独立补充，不倒写成赵州桥比欧洲晚。

### 2022_9_1｜贴春联

Putting up Spring Festival couplets is an important custom through which Chinese people celebrate the festival. A set consists of two poetic lines and a four-character horizontal scroll. The words are written in gold or black on red paper: red symbolizes good luck, while gold represents wealth. The two lines are placed on either side of the front door, with the horizontal scroll above the doorframe. The lines reflect traditional Chinese poetic conventions: they have the same number of characters and related meanings. The horizontal scroll brings out the theme and adds a finishing touch. In concise language, the couplets create vivid images and express good wishes. When families put them up, people know that the Spring Festival has truly begun.

方法：习俗定义、被动、并列与解释、时间从句。新增提醒：`characters` 是汉字数，不是英文字母数；“锦上添花”宜意译而非逐字直译。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“贴春联是……重要习俗。”** → `Putting up Spring Festival couplets is an important custom...` 动作“贴”用动名词短语作主语，`through which` 再说明这是庆祝春节的方式。
2. **“春联由一对诗句和四字横批组成。”** → `A set consists of two poetic lines and a four-character horizontal scroll.` `consists of` 交代组成；`four-character` 作名词前修饰语时用连字符，`character` 不加复数。
3. **“金色或黑色写在红纸上……红色……金色……”** → `The words are written...: red symbolizes..., while gold represents...` 写法用被动，冒号后解释颜色象征；`while` 对照红与金。
4. **“贴在大门左右两侧和门框上方。”** → `The two lines are placed on either side..., with the horizontal scroll above...` 把对联两行与横批的位置分开，避免误解为三者都贴在门框上方。
5. **“诗句体现……两句字数相同、内容相关。”** → `The lines reflect...: they have the same number of characters and related meanings.` 冒号说明“特点”的具体内容；`characters` 指汉字，不是英文词或字母。
6. **“横批凸显主题，更是锦上添花。”** → `The horizontal scroll brings out the theme and adds a finishing touch.` `adds a finishing touch` 意译成语功能，比逐字翻“花”和“锦”自然。
7. **“简洁文字描绘形象、抒发愿望。”** → `In concise language, the couplets create... and express...` 介词短语交代方式，两个动词并列对应描绘与抒发。
8. **“当家家户户贴春联时……春节拉开序幕。”** → `When families put them up, people know...` `When` 引时间从句，主句写人们的感知；`has truly begun` 用现在完成时表示节日已开启。

### 2022_9_2｜印章

Since ancient times, seals have served as proof of identity and symbols of authority in China. They are not only practical but also an art form: an ancient craft combining calligraphy and carving, often appreciated as an independent art alongside painting and calligraphy. From the choice of materials and the techniques of production to the design of the characters, seals offer a rich range of artistic expression. Artists in other countries usually sign their paintings, whereas Chinese artists often stamp their paintings and calligraphy with a seal instead. In this way, the seal becomes part of the work itself and a means of expressing its individuality.

方法：`since`、`not only...but also...`、对比、结果。新增提醒：`seal` 在此指印章／钤印，不是“密封”；`stamp ... with a seal` 比简单的 `put a seal` 清楚。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“自古以来……身份凭证、权力象征。”** → `Since ancient times, seals have served as...` `since` 与现在完成时呼应，表示从古至今的用途；两个 `as` 后的身份名词并列。
2. **“不仅实用……还是集书法雕刻于一体的艺术……”** → `They are not only practical but also an art form: ...` `not only...but also...` 对称连接两个评价；冒号后补充艺术形式的构成，`combining`、`appreciated` 分别表示主动融合与被欣赏。
3. **“从材料选择、工艺到字体设计……美学表现。”** → `From the choice... to the design..., seals offer...` `From...to...` 框住三个环节，主句才落在“具有丰富表现”。`characters` 在这里指印章文字字形。
4. **“外国艺术家签名，而中国艺术家盖章代替。”** → `Artists in other countries..., whereas Chinese artists...` `whereas` 正面呈现对比；`stamp...with a seal` 明确盖章动作，`instead` 表代替签名。
5. **“这样……成为作品组成部分……体现独特性。”** → `In this way, the seal becomes... and a means of...` `In this way` 承接盖章做法；两个名词性表语并列。`individuality` 指作品独特性，不是艺术家的身份。

### 2022_9_3｜中央电视台总部大楼

1. **“位于朝阳区，总建筑面积……”** → `The CCTV Headquarters building is in... and has a total floor area...` 同一建筑用 `is` 写地点、`has` 写建筑面积；`floor area` 不误作占地面积。

3. **“独特造型使其成为景点……每天吸引游客。”** → `Thanks to its unusual shape, it has become..., drawing...` 原因前置，主句结果用现在完成时；`drawing` 是随之而来的游客流，不是“造型”单独作主语。
4. **“创新结构是中外建筑师长期合作的成果。”** → `The building's innovative structure is the result of...` 明确“结构”而非整座建筑是合作成果；`between Chinese and foreign architects` 表合作双方。
5. **“不仅体现环保意识，而且节约材料。”** → `It reflects... and substantially reduces...` 两个具体作用共用主语 `It`，此处应回指创新结构；`reduces the amount... required` 表节省建材。
6. **“专用通道穿过大楼，展示工作室和历史。”** → `A dedicated passage through the building gives...` 选“通道”作主语，`through the building` 后置说明路线；`a view of... and an introduction to...` 区分可看的工作室与可了解的历史。
7. **“在那里……看到故宫等处景色。”** → `Visitors can also enjoy spectacular views...` `also` 衔接上一句通道体验；`views of` 接故宫和北京其他地方。

### 2023_3_1｜徐霞客

Xu Xiake was a renowned geographer of China's Ming dynasty. Over more than 30 years, he traveled through much of the country, mainly on foot, visiting many remote and little-explored areas. He recorded his observations and findings in detail, leaving valuable research material for later generations. By investigating many rivers in person, he corrected errors in written accounts of their sources. He also described in detail how terrain, climate and other factors affected plants, and vividly portrayed local sights, customs and ways of life. Later generations compiled his records into *The Travels of Xu Xiake*, a work that has had a wide influence both in China and abroad.

方法：过去时、方式状语、并列动词、结果。新增提醒：`source of a river` 是河流源头；`later generations` 与 `descendants` 不完全一样。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“徐霞客是……地理学家。”** → `Xu Xiake was a renowned geographer of China's Ming dynasty.` 人物介绍用主系表，朝代放名词后；历史人物用一般过去时。
2. **“三十多年游遍大半中国，主要徒步……偏僻地区。”** → `Over more than 30 years, he traveled..., mainly on foot, visiting...` 时间先行，主干 `he traveled`；`on foot` 写方式，`visiting` 补具体考察地点。
3. **“详细记录……为后人留下资料。”** → `He recorded..., leaving valuable research material...` 主句是记录，`leaving` 表自然结果；“后人”用 `later generations`，不是只指血缘后代的 `descendants`。
4. **“通过实地调查河流……纠正文献水源错误。”** → `By investigating..., he corrected errors...` `By + -ing` 表方法；`their sources` 的 `their` 回指河流，指河流源头。
5. **“描述地形气候影响……描绘名胜和风土人情。”** → `He also described... and vividly portrayed...` 两个过去式谓语并列；`how... affected plants` 处理“因素对植物的影响”，`customs and ways of life` 覆盖风土人情。
6. **“考察记录由后人整理成……产生广泛影响。”** → `Later generations compiled..., a work that has had...` 用主动句明确整理者；同位语 `a work` 指《徐霞客游记》，`has had` 表影响延续至今。

### 2023_3_2｜郑和

Zheng He was one of the most outstanding navigators in Chinese history, showing exceptional wisdom and ability in navigation, diplomacy, military affairs and other fields. He led a large fleet on seven long voyages, visiting many countries and regions around the western Pacific and Indian Oceans and deepening mutual understanding between China and Southeast Asia and East Africa. His voyages greatly advanced China's economic and cultural exchanges with other countries and contributed to regional peace. To commemorate Zheng He and his achievements, July 11—the date on which he first set sail with his fleet—was designated China's Maritime Day.

方法：人物介绍、数字、分词、目的、被动。新增提醒：`mutual understanding` 涉及双方；“定为”需要表达纪念日被设立，而非郑和亲自设立。

#### 逐句解析（样例）

1. **“郑和是……航海家，在航海、外交、军事等领域……”** → `Zheng He was one of the most outstanding navigators in Chinese history, showing exceptional wisdom and ability in navigation, diplomacy, military affairs and other fields.` 主干先写“郑和是一位航海家”，`one of the most...` 后接复数 `navigators`；郑和是历史人物，这里用 `was`。后半句仍在描述郑和，用 `showing...` 接上，不另造一个主语；三个领域依原文逐项保留。
2. **“七次率船队远航，访问……加深相互了解。”** → `He led a large fleet on seven long voyages, visiting many countries and regions around the western Pacific and Indian Oceans and deepening mutual understanding between China and Southeast Asia and East Africa.` 先抓主干“他率船队七次远航”，`seven` 修饰 `voyages`，不是说有七支船队；“访问”“加深了解”是远航期间的动作或结果，接 `visiting... and deepening...`。`mutual` 保留“相互”；末尾若担心 `between China and Southeast Asia and East Africa` 不够清楚，可写 `between China and countries in Southeast Asia and East Africa`。
3. **“下西洋推进中外交流，也为维护区域和平作出贡献。”** → `His voyages greatly advanced China's economic and cultural exchanges with other countries and contributed to regional peace.` 中文的主题是“郑和下西洋”，英文让 `His voyages` 做主语，避免误写成郑和一个人直接“进行所有交流”；两个同等重要的作用用 `advanced... and contributed...` 并列。`economic and cultural` 两项都要保留；此处直接用 `and` 比强行改成分词更清楚。
4. **“为了铭记……7月11日，即首次启程的日子，被定为……”** → `To commemorate Zheng He and his achievements, July 11—the date on which he first set sail with his fleet—was designated China's Maritime Day.` 先识别“为了”是设立纪念日的目的，句首用 `To commemorate...`。主语是“7月11日”，不是郑和；“即首次启程的日子”用破折号作补充说明；“被定为”用 `was designated`。原文没给首次启程的年份，这句就不擅自补一个年份。

### 2023_3_3｜张骞

Zhang Qian was China's first great explorer. Undeterred by danger and hardship, he made two missions to the Western Regions. These journeys opened trade links between China and West Asia and Europe, carried Chinese silk and silk products westward and helped establish the historic Silk Road. He also introduced the customs, geography, cultures and distinctive species of the Western Regions to China's Central Plains, greatly broadening people's horizons. As historians have noted, without Zhang Qian's missions, the Silk Road would not have been opened, nor would the Han dynasty have developed cultural exchanges with the Western Regions or Europe.

#### 逐句解析

1. **“张骞是中国第一个伟大的探险家。”** → `Zhang Qian was China's first great explorer.` 历史人物身份用一般过去时；`first` 保留题目评价，不另外夸大成“唯一”。
2. **“不畏艰险……两次出使西域。”** → `Undeterred by danger and hardship, he made two missions...` 状态短语先行，主句写两次出使；`the Western Regions` 是历史地理称谓，不等同于当代某一行政区。
3. **“开通通商关系……运丝……开拓丝路。”** → `These journeys opened..., carried... and helped establish...` 以“这些出使”作主语串联三项结果。`helped establish` 比“他一人建立”更谨慎；原句动作主体由人物转为出使，属于自然的英语重组。
4. **“同时介绍西域……开阔视野。”** → `He also introduced..., greatly broadening...` `also` 呼应前面的向西交流；分词短语说明介绍知识所产生的结果。
5. **“正如史学家指出，如果没有……就不可能……也不会……”** → `As historians have noted, without..., ... would not have been opened, nor would... have developed...` `without` 设立与过去事实相反的条件，两个结果用 `would not have + 过去分词`；`nor` 置前导致倒装，不可用普通过去时替代这一反事实逻辑。

### 2023_6_2｜城市公共空间

As China's economy grows rapidly and living standards rise steadily, urban residents are asking more of their environment and quality of life. Local governments are paying greater attention to building and improving public facilities to meet these needs. By creating new squares, parks and public green spaces, or redesigning and renovating existing ones, many cities have given residents more places to relax and socialize. Today, government-funded fitness equipment and walking paths can be found in many cities. They have markedly improved conditions for outdoor activity while making the cities more attractive.

方法：背景原因、目的、方式、并列结果。新增提醒：中文长句可分开，但 `by` 后的两个措施都要保留；`public facilities` 与 `public spaces` 不同。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“随着经济发展、生活水平提高……要求越来越高。”** → `As China's economy grows... and living standards rise..., urban residents are asking more...` `As` 引背景变化，主句写居民需求上升；两项背景和结果不能只留其中一项。
2. **“地方政府更加注重……以满足需求。”** → `Local governments are paying greater attention to... to meet...` `pay attention to` 后接动名词 `building and improving`；句尾 `to meet` 表目的。
3. **“通过新建或改造……提供更多休闲社交场所。”** → `By creating... or redesigning..., many cities have given...` `By + -ing` 写两条措施，主干 `cities have given residents more places` 写成果；`relax and socialize` 对应休闲与社交。
4. **“如今……健身器械和步道随处可见。”** → `Today, government-funded... can be found...` `government-funded` 与 `walking paths` 分别承接购置和铺设的设施，地点状语 `in many cities` 收尾；`can be found` 体现广泛可见。
5. **“既改善户外活动条件，又使城市更美丽。”** → `They have markedly improved... while making...` `They` 回指两类设施，主句写活动条件的改善，`while making` 补并行的美化效果。

### 2023_6_3｜城市人居环境

In recent years, China's cities have developed rapidly, and their living environments have improved markedly. Many are exploring paths to high-quality urban development suited to China's conditions. Urban services continue to improve, and standards of governance have risen. China is continuing to restore urban ecosystems, repair and upgrade city functions, renovate aging residential neighborhoods, expand urban greenery and reduce pollution. At the same time, it is systematically improving urban infrastructure, surveying buildings and municipal facilities, and identifying and addressing safety risks. These efforts aim to give residents a better living environment and make cities more beautiful, safer and more livable.

方法：近年来的时态、信息分组、并列措施、目的。新增提醒：本题政策动作密集，难点是保留“普查”“隐患排查整治”等不同措施，而不是把它们全部概括成 `improve cities`。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“近年来……城市发展……人居环境改善。”** → `In recent years, China's cities have developed..., and... have improved...` 时间范围连到现在，用现在完成时；两个变化用并列谓语保留。
2. **“探索中国特色的高质量发展之路。”** → `Many are exploring paths... suited to China's conditions.` `Many` 回指城市，`suited to...` 后置限定道路，不把“中国特色”生硬逐字堆在名词前。
3. **“城市功能完善，治理水平提高。”** → `Urban services continue to improve, and standards of governance have risen.` 两个变化分主语写清；试译以 `services` 概括“功能”，属于一定程度的窄化，复核时留意原文是否强调更广泛的城市功能。
4. **“生态修复、功能修补、旧区改造、绿化、消除污染。”** → `China is continuing to restore..., repair..., renovate..., expand... and reduce...` 把五类政策动作放同一主语下并列，动词与对象配对；`is continuing to` 强调持续实施。
5. **“同时推进基础设施建设、普查、排查整治隐患。”** → `At the same time, it is systematically improving..., surveying..., and identifying and addressing...` `At the same time` 衔接并行措施；`surveying` 对普查，`identifying and addressing` 对排查与整治，不能统统省成“改善设施”。
6. **“努力创造高品质环境，让城市更美丽、安全、宜居。”** → `These efforts aim to give... and make cities...` 把前述措施合称 `These efforts`，`aim to` 明确目的；末尾三个形容词平行。

### 2024_12_1｜北斗卫星导航系统

The successful development of the Beidou Satellite Navigation System is a major scientific and technological achievement China has made since the start of reform and opening up. Through persistent efforts, its developers overcame a series of technical challenges. The system eventually achieved global coverage and high-precision positioning, making China one of the few countries with an independently developed global satellite navigation system. Beidou is now widely used in transportation, disaster relief, weather forecasting, public safety and many other fields. It has also gained broad international recognition and begun providing high-quality services to an increasing number of countries and regions.

方法：`since`、过去成果与现状、结果、列举。新增提醒：`independently developed` 修饰系统研发能力，不能误译成“中国单独使用”；缓存有跨行断词。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“北斗系统成功研制是……重大科技成就。”** → `The successful development... is a major... achievement China has made since...` 把“成功研制”名词化作主语，`China has made` 修饰成就；`since` 引改革开放以来的时间范围。
2. **“研发人员不懈努力，攻克难题。”** → `Through persistent efforts, its developers overcame...` `Through` 表方法或过程，主句落在 `developers overcame`；已完成的攻克用一般过去时。
3. **“实现全球覆盖、高精度定位，使中国成为……”** → `The system eventually achieved..., making China one of...` 两项系统能力并列作宾语，`making` 承接结果。`independently developed` 指自主研制，不是“只有中国能使用”。
4. **“已广泛应用于交通、救援……领域。”** → `Beidou is now widely used in...` 被动突出应用范围；各领域并列，不要把 `disaster relief` 与 `public safety` 混成一个词。
5. **“国际认可，开始提供服务。”** → `It has also gained... and begun providing...` 两个现在完成时动词共用 `has`，说明从过去发展到现在；`an increasing number of` 对应“越来越多”。

### 2024_12_2｜洋山港

Yangshan Port is an important part of Shanghai's shipping center, China's first deep-water port and one of the largest deep-water ports in the world. After nearly 20 years of development, it has become highly automated. The use of digital technology and artificial intelligence has substantially reduced labor costs and carbon emissions. An independently developed terminal management system allows large equipment to be controlled remotely from more than 100 kilometers away. Although the port appears busy, few workers can be seen operating equipment on site, and it can run around the clock. Yangshan Port will continue to develop and make a greater contribution to turning Shanghai into a global shipping center.

方法：同位描述、持续发展、让步、目的。新增提醒：`remotely from more than 100 kilometers away` 不等于设备在港口之外；“见不到人工操作”不宜绝对写成“没有任何工人”。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“洋山港是……组成部分、第一个深水港、最大港之一。”** → `Yangshan Port is... , ... and one of...` 三个身份共用一个 `is`，但各自的范围要保留：上海航运中心、中国首个、世界最大之一；`one of` 后用复数 `ports`。
2. **“近20年发展……高度自动化。”** → `After nearly 20 years of development, it has become highly automated.` 时间背景先行，`has become` 表发展形成的当前状态。
3. **“数字技术和人工智能减少用工成本、碳排放。”** → `The use of... has substantially reduced...` 把“使用”作主语，两个被降低对象并列；`has reduced` 连接技术应用与现有效果。
4. **“自主研发系统……百公里外远程操控设备。”** → `An independently developed... system allows... to be controlled remotely...` 系统是施能者，设备是被操控对象，所以用 `to be controlled`；距离修饰操控位置，不表示设备在百公里外。
5. **“看似繁忙，却见不到人工操作，而且24小时运作。”** → `Although... appears busy, few workers can be seen..., and it can run around the clock.` `Although` 引反差，主句写现场观察与连续运行；试译的 `few workers` 是谨慎概括，不应推成“完全无人”。
6. **“将不断发展，为上海……作贡献。”** → `Yangshan Port will continue to develop and make a greater contribution to turning...` 两个将来动作并列；`contribution to` 后接动名词 `turning`，目标是建设全球航运中心。

### 2024_12_3｜中国航天

Exploring space has long been a dream of the Chinese nation. In 2003, Shenzhou 5 was successfully launched, and Yang Liwei became the first Chinese astronaut to travel into space. In 2008, Shenzhou 7 was launched, and Zhai Zhigang became the first Chinese astronaut to conduct a spacewalk. In recent years, China's space program has entered a period of rapid innovation and development. Space infrastructure has advanced steadily, and the Chinese space station was fully completed in 2022. The rapid growth of China's space program has written a glorious chapter in the nation's history and contributed greatly to the progress of human civilization. In the future, China will take steadier strides farther into space.

方法：明确年份用过去时、近年来的变化、并列、未来。新增提醒：`spacewalk` 与 `travel into space` 是不同成就；不能把空间站建成时间写作“近年来”而不保留 2022 年。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“遨游太空历来是梦想。”** → `Exploring space has long been a dream...` 动名词作主语，`has long been` 强调梦想延续至今。
2. **“2003年神舟五号发射……杨利伟首次飞入太空。”** → `In 2003, Shenzhou 5 was... launched, and Yang Liwei became...` 一个明确年份下并列两件已完成事件，用一般过去时；发射用被动，人物成就用主动。
3. **“2008年神舟七号……翟志刚首次太空行走。”** → `In 2008... was launched, and... became... to conduct a spacewalk.` 结构与上一句平行，方便读者对照；`spacewalk` 与“飞入太空”是不同事件。
4. **“近年来……进入创新发展快车道。”** → `In recent years, China's space program has entered...` “近年来”跨越至今，用现在完成时；抽象比喻“快车道”意译为 `a period of rapid...`。
5. **“基础设施稳步推进，空间站2022年建成。”** → `Space infrastructure has advanced..., and... was fully completed in 2022.` 前半是持续进展，用现在完成时；后半有具体年份，用一般过去时，两种时态按事实切换。
6. **“迅速发展写下辉煌篇章、作巨大贡献。”** → `The rapid growth... has written... and contributed...` 同一主语、两项影响并列，共用现在完成时助动词 `has`。
7. **“未来……更稳、更远。”** → `In the future, China will take steadier strides farther into space.` `will` 表未来，比较级 `steadier`、`farther` 对应“更稳、更远”，保留原句的形象语气。

### 2024_6_1｜传统中式婚礼

Traditional Chinese wedding customs have a long history. A complete set of wedding rituals gradually took shape beginning in the Zhou dynasty, and some are still observed today. Although customs have changed considerably, Chinese-style wedding celebrations remain grand occasions. Venues are carefully decorated, with red—a symbol of joy—as the main color and many objects conveying wishes for the couple's happiness. During the ceremony, the couple bow to Heaven and Earth, to their parents and to each other. They then hold a banquet for their guests and offer toasts to thank them. Many young people still favor traditional Chinese weddings, enjoying their distinctive and beautiful expression of Chinese romance.

方法：从古至今的时间轴、让步、被动、仪式动作并列。新增提醒：`bow to Heaven and Earth` 是题内给定译语；文化动作应按先后顺序表达。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“传统婚礼习俗历史悠久。”** → `Traditional Chinese wedding customs have a long history.` 先用简洁主谓宾概括主题，避免一开始就堆所有仪式细节。
2. **“周朝开始形成完整仪式……有些沿用至今。”** → `A complete set... took shape..., and some are still observed today.` 形成发生于过去，用 `took shape`；沿用至今是当前状态，用 `are still observed`，两种时态按时间分开。
3. **“如今变化很大，但庆典仍隆重。”** → `Although customs have changed..., celebrations remain...` `Although` 显出让步，主句保留“仍然隆重”；不要把“变化”误写成“消失”。
4. **“场地装饰……红色主调……祝福物件。”** → `Venues are carefully decorated, with red... and many objects...` 场地受装饰用被动；`with` 后并列色彩和物件，`a symbol of joy` 是插入解释。
5. **“拜天地、拜父母、对拜。”** → `During the ceremony, the couple bow to...` `bow to` 是题干给定表达，三个对象顺序保留；同一动作只需写一次谓语。
6. **“然后宴请宾客、敬酒致谢。”** → `They then hold a banquet... and offer toasts...` `then` 连接先后步骤，`offer toasts` 对应敬酒，不等于普通“喝酒”。
7. **“年轻人依然钟情……体验中国式浪漫。”** → `Many young people still favor..., enjoying...` 主句写选择传统婚礼，`enjoying` 写体验；`still` 保留“依然”。

### 2024_6_2｜竹子

China produces abundant bamboo and was the first country to develop and use bamboo resources. Bamboo is widely distributed there in many varieties. Its practical uses range from making chopsticks, tables and chairs to building bridges and houses. Chinese people have long loved bamboo; countless writers and artists have made it the subject of literary works and paintings. Its straight stem symbolizes integrity. Its strong vitality and adaptability allow it to survive even in harsh conditions, so it represents perseverance. For thousands of years, bamboo has been regarded as a symbol of the character of the Chinese nation.

方法：举例、象征关系、让步／极端条件、被动。新增提醒：`integrity` 与 `perseverance` 分别对应“正直”“坚韧”，不能混作同一个抽象品质。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“盛产竹子……最早开发利用。”** → `China produces... and was the first country...` 当前产量用现在时，历史先后用过去时；两个谓语并列但时态不同。
2. **“分布广泛，品种丰富。”** → `Bamboo is widely distributed there in many varieties.` 被动式描述分布，`many varieties` 承接品种，不误作“数量很多的竹竿”。
3. **“实用性强，用于筷子、桌椅、桥梁房屋。”** → `Its practical uses range from making... to building...` `range from...to...` 组织两个用途类别；两个 `-ing` 形式平行。
4. **“中国人爱竹，文人以竹创作。”** → `Chinese people have long loved...; countless... have made it the subject...` 分号串接文化情感与创作事实；`have long loved` 强调持续，`made it the subject of` 明确主题关系。
5. **“主干笔直，象征正直品格。”** → `Its straight stem symbolizes integrity.` 把可见特征作主语，动词 `symbolizes` 接抽象品质；`integrity` 不与下句坚韧混淆。
6. **“生命力适应力强，恶劣环境仍生存，寓意坚韧。”** → `Its... allow it to survive even..., so it represents perseverance.` 原因—能力—寓意链条完整；`even` 表极端条件，`so` 引结果。
7. **“几千年来被视为民族品格象征。”** → `For thousands of years, bamboo has been regarded as...` 时间延续至今用现在完成时，被动突出竹子的文化定位。

### 2024_6_3｜扇子

Fans have been loved in China since ancient times. Today they are appreciated more as works of art than used simply to keep cool. Many are beautifully shaped and finely made, bearing exquisite designs of landscapes, flowers, birds and people, and they have considerable artistic value. Many well-known Chinese painters and calligraphers enjoy writing poems and painting on fans to express their artistic taste. Fans are often given as gifts to convey good wishes and sincere feelings. Although their practical function has diminished greatly, fans continue to play an important role in traditional Chinese culture as cultural symbols and art forms.

方法：古今对比、过去分词、举例、让步。新增提醒：`fans` 需靠上下文避免与“粉丝”混淆；“实用功能减弱”不等于“已经没有实用功能”。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“扇子自古深受喜爱。”** → `Fans have been loved in China since ancient times.` `since` 配现在完成时，被动突出扇子受到喜爱；复数 `fans` 在全文物件语境中不会误作粉丝。
2. **“现在不只是纳凉工具，更多供欣赏。”** → `Today they are appreciated more as works of art than used simply to keep cool.` `more...than...` 比较艺术欣赏与实用功能，意思是重心转移，不是完全不用来纳凉。
3. **“造型优美、做工精良、绘有图案、艺术价值高。”** → `Many are... shaped and... made, bearing... and they have...` 前两个被动式形容制作结果，`bearing` 补图案，末尾主句保留价值判断；山水花鸟人物逐类保留。
4. **“画家书法家在扇上作诗绘画，展示品味。”** → `...enjoy writing... and painting... to express...` `enjoy` 后接 `-ing`；`to express` 表目的，明确艺术品味由作品体现。
5. **“作为礼物赠人，表达祝福感情。”** → `Fans are often given as gifts to convey...` 物件作被赠送对象，用被动；`to convey` 表赠礼目的。
6. **“功能减弱，但文化角色仍重要。”** → `Although... has diminished..., fans continue to play...` `Although` 表让步；`has diminished` 是程度下降，`continue` 是文化作用延续，两者不能混写成“失去一切功能”。

### 2025_12_1｜尊老

Respect for older people is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation. Deeply rooted in people's thinking and behavior, it is a widely observed social norm. This virtue remains strong in China today. People from all walks of life are working to foster a culture of respect and support for older residents. Providing them with convenient services has become a shared commitment: for example, communities have opened dedicated dining halls for older people, and public places provide priority seating. The government has also introduced a range of policies to protect their rights and interests. Respect for older people is an important foundation for social harmony and development, helping to cultivate a caring society and advance civility.

方法：抽象名词主语、举例、目的与结果。新增提醒：可迁移最初养老题的 `older people`、`community dining halls`，但本题重点是“尊老”这一社会价值，不只是养老服务设施。

#### 逐句解析（样例）

1. **“尊老是中华民族的传统美德。”** → `Respect for older people is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation.` 先把“尊老”整体转成名词短语 `Respect for older people`，让抽象行为做主语；“是美德”直接用 `is a traditional virtue`，不必为追求复杂句式把每个“是”都换成 `as`。
2. **“深深植根于……是普遍遵守的行为规范和社会准则。”** → `Deeply rooted in people's thinking and behavior, it is a widely observed social norm.` 句首 `Deeply rooted...` 修饰后面的 `it`，即上一句的尊老美德；主干仍是“它是一种社会规范”。“行为规范／社会准则”意思接近，试译合成 `social norm`，避免重复；若要明确“在中国人的思想和行为中”，可写 `in Chinese people's thinking and behavior`。
3. **“在当今中国，这种美德得到广泛传承。”** → `This virtue remains strong in China today.` 主语继续用 `This virtue`，`in China today` 保留地点和时间；试译用“依然盛行”概括“广泛传承”，简洁但弱化了传承动作。若更贴原文，可改成 `This virtue continues to be widely upheld in China today.`
4. **“社会各界积极营造敬老助老的社会氛围。”** → `People from all walks of life are working to foster a culture of respect and support for older residents.` “社会各界”是人的集合，可明确为 `People from all walks of life`；动作是“营造氛围”，所以用 `foster a culture...`。“敬老”“助老”并列为 `respect and support`，不要只译“尊敬”而漏掉“帮助”。
5. **“提供便利服务已成社会共识，例如长者食堂、优先座位。”** → `Providing them with convenient services has become a shared commitment: for example, communities have opened dedicated dining halls for older people, and public places provide priority seating.` “提供便利服务”整件事做主语，用动名词 `Providing...`；`them` 回指老年人。冒号后列两个具体例子，分别保留社区食堂和公共场所优先座位。若考场觉得这句太长，可在 `shared commitment` 后断句，不必强行用一个长句装下所有信息。
6. **“政府还出台政策，为老年人权益提供有力保障。”** → `The government has also introduced a range of policies to protect their rights and interests.` 先写主干“政府出台政策”，再用 `to protect...` 说明政策目的；`also` 承接前文民间与社区的举措。试译的 `protect` 没明说“有力”，若要保留程度，可写 `to provide strong protection for their rights and interests`。
7. **“尊老是和谐发展的基石，有助于培育氛围、推动文明进步。”** → `Respect for older people is an important foundation for social harmony and development, helping to cultivate a caring society and advance civility.` 前半句先立判断主干；后面的“有助于”是这一美德带来的作用，用 `helping to...` 衔接，两个作用再用 `and` 并列。`advance civility` 是概括译法；如强调“整个社会”的进步，可考虑 `promote progress in society as a whole`，但仍须保留“文明”这一层意思。

### 2025_12_2｜自强自立

The Chinese nation values self-reliance, a spirit deeply rooted in Chinese culture and essential to the nation's survival and development. Since reform and opening up began, China has continued to increase investment in research and has made breakthroughs in information technology, artificial intelligence, space engineering and other fields. Its 5G networks cover the country, telemedicine is developing rapidly, and the use of electronic payments is among the world's most advanced. The Chang'e lunar exploration program showcases striking progress in space technology. Self-reliance has become a powerful force driving China's modernization and inspiring its people to work toward the Chinese Dream of national rejuvenation.

方法：抽象概念解释、`since`、领域列举、结果。新增提醒：`self-reliance` 要贯穿前后，`national rejuvenation` 是题内给定关键词；成果列举的主语不应强行统一成一个系统。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“崇尚自强自立……深植文化……支撑生存发展。”** → `The Chinese nation values self-reliance, a spirit deeply rooted...` 主干先写民族重视何种精神；逗号后的同位语解释文化根基和作用。`self-reliance` 在后文重复，保持核心概念一致。
2. **“改革开放以来……科研投入……各领域突破。”** → `Since reform and opening up began, China has continued... and has made...` `since` 接起点，两个现在完成时谓语写持续投入与形成的突破；领域依次列出。
3. **“5G覆盖、远程医疗发展、电子支付领先。”** → `Its 5G networks cover..., telemedicine is developing..., and... is among...` 三个事实分别选合适主语与时态，不能机械地让“中国”做每一项的同一谓语；`among the world's most advanced` 传达领先但没有额外声称绝对第一。
4. **“嫦娥探月展现航天发展。”** → `The Chang'e lunar exploration program showcases...` 独立成句突出航天例子，`showcases` 表“展现”，不凭空增加某一次具体发射事件。
5. **“精神成为动力，激励人民实现中国梦。”** → `Self-reliance has become... driving... and inspiring...` 主干 `has become a powerful force`；两个现在分词分别说明推动现代化与激励人民的作用，`national rejuvenation` 保留题给关键词。

### 2025_12_3｜节俭

Frugality is a traditional virtue of the Chinese nation. In ancient China, productivity was low, and people understood how hard-won the fruits of labor were, so they practiced frugality. Even in today's age of material abundance, Chinese people continue to lead thrifty lives, reflecting a commitment to rational consumption and respect for labor. In recent years, the government has continued to promote a resource-conserving society. It has encouraged the “Clean Plate Campaign” to reduce food waste, advocated simple, low-carbon lifestyles, promoted the recycling of resources and opposed excessive consumption. These efforts have helped drive high-quality economic development in China.

方法：古今对照、原因与结果、政策措施并列。新增提醒：`frugality` 为题内给定；“光盘行动”不译成保存数据的 `disc`。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“节俭是传统美德。”** → `Frugality is a traditional virtue...` 题干给出 `frugality`，直接用主系表定义主题。
2. **“古代生产力低……深知劳动不易……因而节俭。”** → `In ancient China, productivity was low, and... understood..., so... practiced...` 一般过去时构成历史背景—认识—行为的因果链；`so` 清楚承接“因而”。
3. **“物质丰富的今天依然节俭……体现理性消费和尊重劳动。”** → `Even in today's age of material abundance, ... continue..., reflecting...` `Even` 表古今反差，主句写持续生活方式，`reflecting` 表其体现的观念。
4. **“近年来政府加强节约型社会建设。”** → `In recent years, the government has continued to promote...` `In recent years` 常配现在完成时强调持续；`resource-conserving society` 指资源节约型社会。
5. **“倡导光盘、减少浪费、低碳、循环利用、反过度消费。”** → `It has encouraged..., advocated..., promoted... and opposed...` 四个动词共用 `It has`；`to reduce food waste` 明确光盘行动目的，不把“光盘”误译成数据载体。
6. **“节约型社会建设推动经济高质量发展。”** → `These efforts have helped drive...` `These efforts` 汇总上文措施；`helped drive` 表推动作用，不把单一措施说成发展唯一原因。

### 2025_6_1｜天宫空间站【待核术语】

The Tiangong Space Station, China's first space laboratory, has more than 110 cubic meters of usable space. It can accommodate three astronauts and orbits 400 to 450 kilometers above Earth. More than 180 scientific research and application projects have been carried out there, covering space life sciences, space medicine, space materials science and other fields. Research findings from the station have been widely applied in China, generating substantial economic benefits. For example, space breeding has produced direct economic benefits amounting to hundreds of billions of yuan. These achievements not only mark major advances in China's space technology but also demonstrate its significant contribution to global space research and applications.

方法：空间与轨道数字、现在完成时、举例、`not only...but also...`。**术语提醒：上段是对缓存的试译，不能把“首个太空实验室”作为已核实事实。** [中国载人航天官方网站](https://www.cmse.gov.cn/fxrw/tgyhyszbhrw/jskd/201110/t20111003_38262.html)称天宫一号为我国首个空间实验室；同一机构把[天宫空间站](https://www.cmse.gov.cn/gygc/xtzc/kjzxt/)称作空间实验平台、国家级太空实验室。应核对原卷是否为 OCR 或源文用语问题。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“天宫空间站是首个太空实验室，拥有110多立方米空间。”** → `The Tiangong Space Station, China's first space laboratory, has...` 同位语承接缓存原文说法，主干是 `station has...`。但“首个太空实验室”的史实/术语疑点已在上方提示，考试背诵前须核对原卷。
2. **“可驻留三名宇航员……400–450公里轨道。”** → `It can accommodate three astronauts and orbits...` 两个能力共用 `It`；`above Earth` 表离地高度，勿与轨道长度混淆。
3. **“已实施180多个项目，涉及三个领域。”** → `More than 180... projects have been carried out there, covering...` 项目作被实施对象，用现在完成时被动；`covering` 后列举研究领域。
4. **“成果广泛应用……产生显著经济效益。”** → `Research findings... have been widely applied..., generating...` 被动突出成果的应用范围，`generating` 引出经济效益。
5. **“例如太空育种……数千亿元。”** → `For example, space breeding has produced... hundreds of billions of yuan.` `For example` 使例证层级清晰；“数千亿”以 `hundreds of billions` 表示数量级，不误成数十亿。
6. **“不仅标志技术进步，也表明全球贡献。”** → `These achievements not only mark... but also demonstrate...` `not only...but also...` 连接两个并列判断；`These achievements` 回指前面的项目及成果，而不是单指太空育种。

### 2025_6_2｜粤港澳大湾区

The Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area has an advantageous location, well-developed transport infrastructure and rich industrial resources. It is one of China's most open and economically dynamic regions and holds an important strategic position in national development. The area is expected to become both a vibrant world-class city cluster and an international center for scientific and technological innovation with global influence. It will also develop into a high-quality living area where people can work, shop and travel. As reform and opening up continues to deepen, its development will further boost the regional economy. By 2035, the area aims to become a world-class bay area.

方法：`one of...`、并列目标、时间界限与发展愿景。新增提醒：`by 2035` 是目标期限，不能译成已经实现；题内给定英文名称应优先采用。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“地理位置优越，交通完善、产业资源丰富。”** → `The Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area has...` 采用题给专名，`has` 统领三项优势；三个名词短语并列，避免频繁重复 `there is`。
2. **“开放程度最高、活力最强区域之一，战略地位重要。”** → `It is one of China's most... regions and holds...` `one of` 后接复数 `regions`，两个最高级限制在“中国区域之一”；`holds` 承接战略地位。
3. **“将建成世界级城市群、国际科技创新中心。”** → `The area is expected to become both... and...` `both...and...` 并列两项目标；试译 `is expected to` 带预期语气，比原文“将”稍弱，若强调确定规划可改用 `will`，但此处保留原试译。
4. **“还将打造成适合工作购物旅游的生活圈。”** → `It will also develop into... where people can...` `also` 连接第三项目标；`where` 后三个动词写明生活圈功能，不只写抽象“宜居”。
5. **“随着改革开放深入……进一步推动区域经济。”** → `As... continues to deepen, its development will further boost...` `As` 引背景趋势，`will` 写未来结果；`further` 保留“进一步”。
6. **“到2035年实现国际一流湾区目标。”** → `By 2035, the area aims to become...` `By` 是截至某期限，`aims to` 表目标而非已实现；原文“一流”译为 `world-class`，这是常见概括。

### 2025_6_3｜南水北调工程

China's water resources have long been unevenly distributed, with the north facing shortages and the south having abundant supplies. To address the severe water shortage in the north, the government launched the South-to-North Water Diversion Project, a massive engineering undertaking that transfers water across regions. After decades of planning and preparation, construction began in 2002. The project consists of eastern, central and western routes totaling 4,350 kilometers and is expected to benefit more than 400 million people. Since water began flowing through it in 2014, it has transferred more than 50 billion cubic meters of water to the north. It has provided people there with a reliable water supply, greatly improved the region's environment and promoted sustainable, healthy economic development.

方法：南北对比、目的、时间轴、三路线、累计量与结果。新增提醒：`累计已超` 应保留累计概念和立方米单位；“将惠及”是预期，不应写为全部已惠及。

#### 逐句解析

1. **“自古北缺南丰，分布不均。”** → `China's water resources have long been unevenly distributed, with the north... and the south...` 主句总述分布，`with` 结构再对照南北；`have long been` 把长期状态延续到现在。
2. **“为了缓解北方缺水……实施工程。”** → `To address..., the government launched...` 不定式前置表目的，主干 `the government launched...`；工程名称采用题给英文。
3. **“这是一项跨区域配置水资源的宏大工程。”** → `..., a massive engineering undertaking that transfers water across regions.` 用同位语附在工程名称后，`that` 从句解释其功能，避免重复 `This is`。
4. **“历经数十年筹备，2002年开工。”** → `After decades of planning and preparation, construction began in 2002.` 时间背景在前，主句把“开工”译成 `construction began`，不用无主句。
5. **“分东中西三线，总长4350公里，将惠及4亿多人。”** → `The project consists of... routes totaling... and is expected to benefit...` 路线、里程与预期受益人数依次说明；`is expected to benefit` 保留“将”，不提前当作已经完成。
6. **“2014年通水以来累计向北调水超500亿立方米。”** → `Since water began flowing... in 2014, it has transferred more than 50 billion cubic meters...` `since` 指起点、现在完成时指累计到现在；`cubic meters` 是体积单位，500亿 = 50 billion。
7. **“提供可靠水源、改善生态、促进经济发展。”** → `It has provided..., greatly improved... and promoted...` 三个结果共用现在完成时助动词 `has`；对应民生、生态、经济三个层面，顺序与中文一致。

## 覆盖结论与下一轮补充重点

38 篇都能用“找主干—辨逻辑—断句—核对信息”的框架动笔；但这只说明**方法入口覆盖**，不等于词汇、专名、搭配和最终得分全部覆盖。四篇课堂示例直接对应缓存题（`2020_12_1`、`2020_12_2`、`2021_6_1`、`2021_12_2`）；另有 `2025_12_1` 与最初养老主题相近，但不是同一道题。

下一轮最值得补的不是更多“万能句型”，而是以下四类可复用难点：

1. **数字、单位、范围与时间轴。** 大兴机场、各类高原、桥梁、空间站和调水工程集中出现；核对“截至／自……以来／到……将”、`from...to...`、比较范围及单位。
2. **文化与历史题材的词汇和专名。** 四大名著、春节、印章、婚礼、竹子、扇子及历史人物，需要按含义选词，避免为了“高级”而失真。
3. **逻辑类型多于目的关系。** `2023_3_3` 的反事实、`2024_6_1` 的让步、`2023_6_2` 的方式、`2022_9_2` 的对比，都不能套成统一的 `to do` 或分词尾巴。
4. **长政策句的信息保全。** `2023_6_3`、`2025_12_3`、`2025_6_2` 的措施与目标需分组表达，既不断章取义，也不机械串联十几个动作。

待核源文索引：`2020_9_1`（“玄奖”“取竺”），`2021_12_2`（“黄河中叶”），`2021_12_3`（人物与日期的史实关系），`2022_12_1`（“珍稀野生成动物”），`2025_6_1`（“首个太空实验室”的用语）。另有 `2020_7_1`、部分 2024/2025 题存在跨行断词，但本轮未见其直接改变语义。以上均未写回缓存。
